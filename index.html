⚡ Why order matters

A large reward earned in transaction #1 may lower cash required for transaction #2. The calculator compares all permutations of up to six selected offers and favors the lowest overall cash outlay. It does not repeat a weekly-limited promotion or transfer rewards to another retailer.

How updates work

The scheduled collector checks publicly available RSS feeds, identifies retailer-specific leads, and records additions/changes. Only separately verified deals with current-day dates and complete basket math may appear in the numerical rankings. RSS omission does not prove expiration. Some retailers block automated access, and personalized coupons require your own account verification.

The static website itself cannot perform unrestricted background scraping. Automatic daily updates start only after the included repository is deployed and its scheduled GitHub Actions workflow runs successfully.

How rankings are computed

Effective cost = pay at checkout − store rewards − external rebates
Savings % = (regular − effective) ÷ regular × 100

Rewards are shown individually so you can distinguish discounts today from credit you may use later. We never treat a $10 ExtraBucks award as $10 immediate cash off.

What we verify—and cannot yet

  • ✓ Published weekly-ad prices, deal quantities and dates
  • ✓ Identified manufacturer, store and external rebate components
  • ✓ Obvious threshold mistakes and suspicious coupon mismatches flagged
  • ◌ Your account-specific clipping and personalized offers
  • ◌ Knoxville store inventory, precise UPC and checkout total
  • ◌ Automatic scraping or continuous background updates

💡 Case study: the $0.60 Kroger “money-maker”

An Idahoan potatoes pouch is shown at $0.99 with a potential $1.59 Ibotta rebate. However the 99¢ Kroger price requires buying at least five eligible sale items. Our ranked deal includes the five-item basket ($4.95 upfront) rather than falsely claiming the single potatoes item can be purchased alone for a 60¢ gain.
